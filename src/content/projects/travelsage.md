## The problem

Planning a trip with friends usually means a group chat, a spreadsheet for costs, and a notes app for the packing list. Plans drift, nobody knows who owes what, and the latest version lives in someone's head.

## What I built

TravelSage puts the whole trip in one place that everyone can edit at once.

- **Itinerary.** Day-by-day activity planning with drag-to-reorder, time conflict detection, a timeline view, and booking attachments.
- **Collaborators.** Invite people by email as an editor or viewer. Google sign-in makes joining a trip quick.
- **Expenses.** Shared and personal expenses with multi-currency support, equal splits, a balance summary, and a settle-up view.
- **Packing lists.** A shared checklist for the trip, plus a personal list for each person.
- **Notes.** Notes for the whole trip or for a single day.
- **Multi-destination trips.** Days can be grouped by destination for multi-city trips.
- **Realtime sync.** Changes from collaborators show up live, with no refresh.
- **Shareable link.** A read-only view of the trip for people without an account.
- **Dark mode and PDF export.**

## The result

One shared plan replaces the group chat, the spreadsheet, and the notes app, and everyone can see the itinerary, what they owe, and what to pack.
